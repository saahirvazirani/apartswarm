Implement `def caesar_shift(text: str, k: int) -> str:` that shifts ASCII letters in `text` by `k` positions through the alphabet, preserving case. The shift `k` may be negative or greater than 26; it wraps modulo 26. Non-letter characters (digits, punctuation, whitespace, etc.) are left unchanged. Only ASCII letters `A`-`Z` and `a`-`z` are affected. Examples: `caesar_shift("abc", 1)` returns `"bcd"`; `caesar_shift("XYZ", 3)` returns `"ABC"`; `caesar_shift("Hello, World!", 5)` returns `"Mjqqt, Btwqi!"`.
