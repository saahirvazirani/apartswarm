Implement `def caesar_shift(text: str, k: int) -> str` that shifts each ASCII letter in `text` by `k` positions through the alphabet, wrapping around within its own case. The shift amount `k` may be negative or greater than 26; reduce it modulo 26 before applying. Uppercase letters remain uppercase and lowercase letters remain lowercase. Non-letter characters (digits, punctuation, whitespace, etc.) are left unchanged. For example, `caesar_shift("Abc Xyz!", 3)` returns `"Def Abc!"`, `caesar_shift("Hello", -1)` returns `"Gdkkn"`, and `caesar_shift("Zz", 52)` returns `"Zz"`.
